Ye Olde Farm Lodge

Escape to the charm and tranquility of Ye Olde Farm Lodge, a rustic historic farmhouse in conveniently located French Creek, NY.

Ye Olde Farm Lodge is a 5-bedroom, fully-furnished rental available for daily, weekly, and weekend stays. Guests can enjoy spacious grounds ideal for family picnics, reunions, and private events with amenities including volleyball courts, horseshoe pits, and a large outdoor fire pit.

The property sits just a mile from the renowned Peek’n Peak Resort, placing year-round recreation quite literally at your doorstep. In the winter months, the slopes come alive with skiing, snowboarding, and tubing, while the warmer seasons give way to rolling greens, miniature golf, zip-lining through the treetops, and evenings filled with live entertainment under open skies.

Beyond the resort, adventure stretches even further. Ye Olde Farm Lodge offers direct access to Chautauqua County’s expansive network of more than 400 miles of groomed snowmobile trails - an ever-winding path through pristine woods, open fields, and snow-draped countryside. Whether by ski, sled, golf cart or trail, the land invites you to step outside and into the season itself!
