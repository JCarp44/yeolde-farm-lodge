Ye Olde Farm Lodge

A peaceful countryside escape

Welcome

Escape to the charm and tranquility of Ye Olde Farm Lodge, a rustic historic farmhouse located just down the road from Peek'n Peak Resort and many popular area destinations.

Ye Olde Farm Lodge is a 5-bedroom, fully-furnished rental available for daily, weekly, and weekend stays. Guests can enjoy spacious grounds ideal for family picnics, reunions, and private events with amenities including volleyball courts, horseshoe pits, and a large outdoor fire pit.
